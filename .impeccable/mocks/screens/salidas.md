# salidas — approval queue for outgoing Slack/Basecamp messages
What: queue (Por firmar / Enviadas hoy / Denegadas / Caducadas) + detail. Exact destination drawn as a route in the source colour (Slack: Workspace -> Canal -> En el hilo de -> Publica como; Basecamp: Proyecto -> recording or Campfire -> Como). Then "Texto que se enviará" = the current version, a version stepper (v1 · v2 · v3, previous one dashed = compared) with a one-line diff vs the previous version, Aprobar y enviar vN / Editar / Denegar, and the refine chat.
Decision 2026-11-03 (Andrés): "salida debe tener un chat porque muchas veces pasa que lo que me escribes no me gusta y hacemos back and forward hasta que elgamos a algo que me gusta". Added "Afinar con Claude": compact turns (Tú: "más corto" -> Claude: v2), input "Pídele un cambio a Claude…" + Enviar, quick chips Más corto · Más formal · En inglés · Sin emojis.
Variants: A Cola + detalle (recommended; chat sits under the actions in the 560px pane). B Una por una: two-column card, text + actions left, chat right, A/E/D shortcuts.
States: Claude reescribiendo… (pending v4 dashed in Claude orange; Aprobar and Editar disabled, Cancelar el cambio), editando (manual edit over vN with diff), enviando, enviado (permalink), falló, denegada, caducada, Basecamp comment and Campfire destinations.
Many versions (Andrés, 2026-11-03: "ok que pasa cuando hay muchas versiones?"): stepper collapses to v1 · … · starred · … · last three ([v9] current); "…" opens a version list (number, star, the turn that produced it, age) where any two can be ticked to diff; starred versions ("me gustaba esta") always stay in the stepper; "Volver a v4" makes it current, so Aprobar sends v4; older turns fold into "6 vueltas anteriores · ver", last 2 stay open; after 8 versions a quiet "¿Lo escribes tú? Editar a mano". States: Muchas versiones, lista abierta, Volviste a v4.
Semáforo (Andrés, 2026-11-03: "me gustaria que pudiera seleccionar como en semaforo diferentes palabras o frases verde como lo que no vayas a cambiar amarillo sinonimos u otra version y rojo se va completamente"): selecting text in "Texto que se enviará" opens a floating toolbar Mantener / Reformular / Quitar / Limpiar (both variants). Shape carries the meaning because red/green/orange are source lines: Mantener = solid underline + lock + green tint, Reformular = wavy underline + yellow tint, Quitar = strikethrough + red tint. New tokens --keep-*/--redo-*/--drop-* (tint + ink) in Tokio and Grafito; text stays --ink on the tints (AA). Marks ride with the chat's own Enviar (Andrés: "esto no deberia estar al recomendacion se manda con el mismo boton de enviar"): no separate button and no legend under the text; while marks exist an attachment chip sits above the chat input, "Con marcas: 3 mantener · 2 reformular · 1 quitar ×" (× clears them). Enviar sends typed text + marks as one Tú turn, works with marks and no text, and consumes the marks. While marks exist the inline-diff line is hidden. Next version: green marks persist on identical text, yellow show old struck + new wavy, red shown gone. State block id="semaforo": marked v3 and resulting v4. Interactive (Andrés: "pero la idea es poder seleccionar palabras o frases o sea arrastrando"): drag/double-click/touch selection in a [data-sema] block snaps to whole words and shows the fixed toolbar above it; marks are <mark class="m-keep|m-redo|m-drop">, rebuilt from a per-character model so overlaps split/replace cleanly; Limpiar unwraps touched marks; K/R/Q/Esc; chip counts live (hidden at 0). Handlers ignore events inside the impeccable live bar.
Origen (Andrés, 2026-11-03: "ahora normalmente los mensajes de salida son el resultado final de una investigacion o una serie de desarrollos por ende deberia estar por ahi el historial del ticket para ver a que esta relacionado"): compact strip at the top of the detail with the ticket that produced it (Ticket a17) and its linked sources as line chips joined by interchange rings (PR #59 · Slack #scout-dev · Basecamp · Scout), plus "Historial 9" toggle. Historial is collapsed by default; open, it is a vertical line of stations (time, source-colour dot, one line, link), newest last, "+3 anteriores" on top, each station a <details> with its evidence (log line, PR comment, Slack message). Narrow pane (A): history sits below the text and actions; wide card (B): right column above the chat. Destination + text stay first. State block id="origen" shows it open.
Chosen: B (Andrés, 2026-11-03: "me gusta la B, solo que no me restringa el orden o sea que si no quiero responder esa aun que pueda elegir otra y darle"). B is the default view (body data-view="b"). No forced sequence: a queue strip above the card lists every pending outgoing message (source line, destination, first line, age, expiry) and any one opens by click; J/K or arrows move without acting; A/E/D act on the open one; "Saltar por ahora" (S) moves it to the end without deciding; the counter reads "3 pendientes", no "1 de 3". Deciding opens the next undecided item but every item stays clickable; decided items stay in the strip dashed with Enviada/Denegada.
Memoria (Andrés, 2026-11-03: "me gustaria que la otra mitad de esta seccion sea un vistazo a las decisiones de engram asi como se muestran en los boards de nexo"): in B the right column splits into two equal halves, each scrolling; top = Historial + Afinar con Claude as before, bottom = "Memoria · engram" modeled on nexo MemoryPanel: status dot (solid = conectado, dashed ring = sin conexión), "Buscar en la memoria" filter, up to 5 observations matched from the ticket title, each type chip (decision solid ink, lesson outline, process dashed, board underlined square) + title + age, titles and ages only, never bodies. Decisions that support a phrase in the draft are flagged "aplica a este mensaje" with a 45-degree link stub to the phrase; hover/focus outlines that phrase in the text. Footer "Registrado en engram · board/a17 · sincronizado hace 1 min" stays pinned. No line colours in the panel (lines carry source only). States: engram caído (id memoria-caida, signing never blocked) and nada relacionado (id memoria-vacia; also live on queue item 3).
Revertir (Andrés, 2026-11-03: "tambien quiero una opcion de revertir el envio es decir eliminar el mensaje"): every source gets a 20 s undo window. Aprobar y enviar puts the item in "Enviando en 20 s · Deshacer" with its tick travelling along the source line to the destination station (5 jumps under reduced motion); Deshacer or Z returns it to pending with the same version; the send only happens when the countdown ends, then the next pending item opens. Sent state shows the permalink plus a delete control per destination: Basecamp comment = "Eliminar mensaje", confirm "Se mueve a la papelera de Basecamp · recuperable 25 días", then "Eliminado · Restaurar"; Campfire line = "Eliminar", confirm warns it is permanent; Slack = "Eliminar" disabled with "Slack no permite borrar desde Mesa todavía · ábrelo en Slack" and an "Abrir en Slack" link, because the Slack connector has no delete or edit tool (a sent Slack message cannot be removed from Mesa), so for Slack the undo window is the only way back and the countdown says so. States: Enviando · deshacer, Deshecho, Slack: no se puede eliminar, Eliminado (Basecamp).
Approve button: option 3 chosen (Andrés, 2026-11-03: "el 3"). Split button "Aprobar y enviar v3 | ▾" is the default on every approve button in A, B and the #boton-aprobar block; the main half sends with the 20 s Deshacer window, ▾ opens "Enviar en 20 s" (default, ticked) and "Programar…" (key P in B). Phone A: same split in the dock; phone B (half-width Aprobar): long-press opens a sheet with the same two options. Programado: picking a time (Hoy 16:00 · mié 9:00 · mar 9:00) shows "Sale mar 9:00 · Cambiar · Cancelar", locks the text as "Texto programado · sale …", keeps the item in the queue strip with a clock mark and the counter adds "· 1 programada"; Cancelar returns it to pending. State block "Programado".
Quick chips are learned (Andrés, 2026-11-03: "no, no quiero eso quiero que sean dinamicos en funcion a lo que yo pido en los cambios frecuentemente" and "si, pero resumido me entiendes?"): the chips under the chat input are only his own frequent change requests, taken automatically from the refine turns he sent and grouped by meaning into one 1-3 word label ("acórtalo", "menos texto, porfa" -> "Más corto"; "quita el hola", "sin el saludo" -> "Sin saludo"). Up to 4, ranked by how often he asked; hover/focus shows "agrupa: …" with 1-2 original phrasings; clicking sends the label (with any semáforo marks, like Enviar). No presets, no per-destination sets, no manual manager or pinning. Cold start: no chips, only "Aquí aparecerán tus pedidos frecuentes". State block id="chips" shows both.
Must not change: Aprobar always sends the version shown (label carries the number); never approvable while a rewrite is pending; destination stays visible above the text at every version.
Devs: each chat request creates an immutable version on the draft; stepper selects which one is shown (and therefore sent); inline diff is vs the previous version, the list compares any two; stars are per draft. Editar is a manual edit, not a chat turn, and produces "tu versión". Exact-text block uses a top line stub, not hoy.html's 4px left border.
