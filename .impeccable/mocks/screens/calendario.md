# calendario — your day's meetings, a free-text way to ask for one, and a warning before each
What: a "Calendario" screen in the rail (after Salidas), a "Próxima reunión" strip in Hoy and on the phone, and a reminder 10 min before each meeting. Read-only toward Google (a private iCal URL polled by the Mesa server, about every 5 min); creating an event is a salida that the lead makes through the Google Calendar connector after Andrés signs.
Colour (decided 2026-11-11, "B"): Calendario is the FIFTH source line colour, indigo `--ca` (light #5b5bd6, text `--ca-ink` #3d3dab, tint `--ca-tint` #e9e8fb; Grafito #8a8aff, #b3b3ff, #23233f). It sits far from teal (hue 182), green (162), red (355) and orange (37) in both themes, is 4.5:1 on porcelain as a mark and 7:1 as text, and always travels with the word "Calendario" or a time. It is the strip's line and ring, the week band's line, the agenda's next-row rule, the free-slot bar and the proposed (dashed) slot, the health pill's line, the chips and the top stub of the calendar salida, and the Calendario rail stub on the phone. Calendar events are not items, so Calendario is not a line of the Hoy band. DESIGN.md: "the five line inks mean source only".
Idiom: the next meeting is a line that runs into an open ring (Calendario line -> ink-outlined ring). Source health is the `Google Calendar · leído hace 3 min` pill; unreachable or unconnected turns it dashed with a dotted line. A proposed slot is a dashed outline in the Calendario colour; busy time is a solid Calendario mark; a declined meeting is a dashed `mute` outline.
Variants:
- A Agenda + pedir (recommended). Week as a line band (stations are the days, today burns, the rest of the week drops to 35% like the Hoy band, counts under each day), the selected day as rows (hora, reunión, personas, Unirse), and a 392px pane to ask for a meeting in free text with the last request's thread and a free-slot bar. Same grammar as Hoy, Tickets and Salidas A; the phone is the same list.
- B Semana en rejilla. Mon-Fri by hour with the current-time line, the proposed slot drawn as a dashed block in the grid, and the request as a bar docked at the bottom. Best for seeing free slots at a glance; worse for scanning one day and has no phone form (the phone falls back to the agenda).
Strip "Próxima reunión": porcelain, 1px `rule`, 64px; title 16/700, "en 18 min · 15:00–15:30 · 4 personas", Unirse (primary) and Abrir. At 10 min or less the border turns ink with Float low; "En curso" shows "termina HH:MM"; with nothing left it is dashed "Sin más reuniones hoy". Not connected: the strip does not show.
Phone: bottom tab bar gains Calendario (5 tabs, 78px each). Calendario shows the strip (Unirse 52px full width), day chips Lun-Vie with counts, 62px rows (hora, title, personas, Meet button) and "Pedir reunión" docked at 58px. Push: "Reunión en 10 min · 15:00" with the meeting title as the body; tapping opens the Meet link, or the event in Mesa when there is none.
Reminder rules (decisions 5A, 6C): 10 min before start, PC notification and phone Web Push, both with the title. This is an exception to the t33 "push carries no titles" rule for meeting reminders only; every other push keeps the rule. They fire even with a Mesa tab focused (meetings bypass the quiet rule), once per event start (a moved start notifies again), never for all-day events or declined meetings.
Salida (4A): every event is a salida, with or without invitees. Detail pane: "Destino exacto" chips (calendar, Google Meet sí/no, N invitees), context quote, "Evento que se creará" exact-text block (title, cuándo, zona America/Bogota, invitados, Meet), a check line "Comprobado contra tus eventos guardados: sin choques", Aprobar y crear / Editar / Denegar, and "Matt recibirá una invitación de Google. 20 s para deshacer.".
States: sin conectar (names the env file, never shows the URL), lectura fallida (alert, last stored data keeps reminders alive), día sin reuniones, all-day row without reminder, rechazada, en curso.
Must not change: nothing is created in Google without a released salida; the iCal URL never reaches the browser, a log or an API response; no semantic red or green, and no colour for "busy" or "free": mark weight and dashes carry it.
Devs: calendar events are read from stored rows, never from the browser; the free-slot check runs on stored events (Bogota); sample events only through the seed, marked `isSample`, shown with "Datos de muestra.".
